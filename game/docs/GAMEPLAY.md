# Gameplay

Choose Mathematics or English and a difficulty. Enter the dark East Wing, explore with WASD/arrows while visibility is limited, find the red terminal, press E, and answer one subject-specific question. The result screen saves a local score.

Planned slices include inventory, keys, multiple rooms, audio cues, hiding, environmental notes, and creature AI. The current scene is intentionally small so those systems can be extracted without coupling them to the platform UI.
